WEDDIES

Privacy Policy

English Nederlands Deutsch

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Weddies is an iPhone app that shows football matches near you and lets you keep a logbook of the grounds you have visited. This policy explains which data Weddies uses, why, where it is stored, how long it is kept and what your rights are.

In short

  • Weddies works fully without an account. Without one, your logbook stays on your iPhone and Weddies collects no personal data.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your data is never sold or used for advertising.
  • Your location is only used with your permission, only while you use the app, and is not stored by Weddies.
  • An account and Friends are optional. If you use them, your data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt), and you can delete your account at any time in the app.

1. Who is responsible

Weddies is made by Jasper Havers, an independent developer in the Netherlands. He is the controller of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can reach him at kithavers@gmail.com.

3. Using Weddies without an account

Your logbook

  • Your visits, ratings, prices, notes, beers, photos, own matches and own clubs are stored only on your iPhone.
  • They are included in your iPhone's regular iCloud backup if you have that turned on (Apple's privacy policy applies). The developer cannot access them.

Your location

  • Weddies only uses your location if you allow it, and only while you are using the app.
  • It is used to show matches near you, to calculate distances and, for "Fancy a match?", to work out travel times.
  • Weddies does not store your location and never sends it to the developer. Travel times, place searches (for example the ground of a match you add yourself or the starting point of a hop trip) and place names are handled by Apple Maps, which receives the locations involved. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • You can change or withdraw this permission at any time in the iOS Settings app (Weddies → Location).

Camera and photos

  • The camera is only used when you take a photo yourself. You choose photos with the iOS photo picker; Weddies only sees the photos you pick.
  • Photos of your visits stay on your iPhone and are never uploaded, not even with an account.

Sharing

  • Story images are created on your iPhone. You share them yourself using the iOS share button (for example to Instagram). Weddies never sends anything automatically.

4. Account (optional)

  • An account is entirely voluntary. Without one, Weddies works exactly the same; your logbook then stays only on your phone.
  • You sign in with Apple or with a 6-digit code sent by email. There is no password.
  • What is stored: your email address (or Apple's relay address if you choose "Hide My Email"), your name if Apple provides it, an account ID and the sign-in methods you use. Our hosting provider Supabase keeps technical sign-in records (such as time and IP address) for security.
  • Your logbook in your account: visits, ratings, prices, notes, number of beers, ticked-off grounds, own matches, own clubs and grounds, corrected ground locations, and the settings "my club(s)", counting rule and "hide beer". Your photos are not included; they stay on your phone.
  • Why: only to keep your logbook safe and show it on your other phones (for example after switching to a new phone). Never for advertising, and never shared or sold.
  • Who can see it: only you. Database rules (Row Level Security) make sure only you can read and change your data. The developer has technical admin access, but only looks at your data when you ask (for example for support) or when required by law.
  • Your email address is only used to send you the sign-in code. That email is sent by Supabase through the email service Resend. No newsletters, no marketing.
  • Deleting your account: at any time in the app via Profile → Delete account. Everything in your account is then deleted immediately, including your username, profile photo and friendships. The logbook on your phone stays. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

5. Friends (optional, requires an account)

  • You choose a username. Others can find you by it, but in search results they only see that username. An invite link you share contains your username.
  • Only after you accept a friend request (or the other person accepts yours) do you see each other's profile photo, username, club, number of grounds, matches and countries, badges, progress per league and map of grounds. With the switch "Friends see my planned matches" (on by default) they also see which matches you plan to attend at the stadium.
  • Friends never see your ratings, notes, prices or photos of visits.
  • A profile photo is optional. It is resized to 400 × 400 pixels and stored in private storage at Supabase in the EU; only you and your friends can see it.
  • You can remove a friend, block someone (they then no longer see anything of you and can no longer find you or send you a request) and report someone at any time. A report contains your account, the reported account and username, the reason and your optional explanation. The developer reviews reports within 24 hours and can remove content or accounts that break the rules. Reports are kept as long as needed to handle them and prevent repeated abuse.
  • This data is only used to make Friends work: not for advertising, and never shared or sold.

6. Player pages

  • When you tap a player, the app fetches that player's details (photo, date of birth, club, statistics) from a Weddies server at Supabase in the EU, which requests them from the football data service API-Football (API-Sports). Only the player's number is sent, nothing about you.
  • To prevent abuse, a one-way hashed (irreversible) version of your IP address is kept for at most 2 hours.
  • What you personally have with a player ("You saw him live 7 times") is calculated on your phone and is not sent anywhere.

7. Match data, logos and photos

  • The app loads matches, scores and tables from Weddies' storage at Supabase (EU), with a copy on GitHub Pages as a backup. Club and league logos and player photos are loaded from api-sports.io.
  • As with any website, these servers receive technical data such as your IP address when the app downloads something. Weddies does not send any other data, and no identifier. The providers may keep this in their logs for a short time for security and troubleshooting.

8. Reporting to Weddies (optional)

  • If you add a match yourself, or correct a score, kick-off time or ground, you can choose "Report this to Weddies" (or turn on "Report automatically"). The app then sends only football details: the clubs, the ground and its location, the league, the score, the kick-off time and the match number.
  • Nothing about you is sent: no name, no email address, no device and not where you are.
  • Reports go to a Weddies database at Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt). Like any website, Supabase sees your IP address; Weddies does not store it. Reports are only used to improve the match and ground data in Weddies.

9. Feedback and contact

  • Feedback you send through "Feedback" is an ordinary email from your own mail app to the developer. You see the email before you send it. By default it includes the app version, iOS version, iPhone model and language; you can turn that off.
  • Your email is only used to answer you and improve Weddies, is kept in the developer's mailbox (Gmail) only as long as needed and is never shared.
  • If you choose an Instagram message instead, Instagram's privacy policy applies.

10. Weddies Plus (purchases)

  • Weddies Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Weddies never sees your name, email address or payment details.
  • The app only asks the App Store on your iPhone whether you have Plus. Nothing is sent to a Weddies server.
  • You manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

11. Links to other services

  • Some buttons open another app or website, for example a Google search for tickets, a route in Apple Maps or Instagram. Weddies only passes on what is needed for that link (such as the club names). From then on, that service's own privacy policy applies.

12. No advertising, no tracking

  • Weddies contains no advertising, no analytics or statistics services and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across other apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier.

13. Service providers

Weddies uses a few service providers. They only process data on Weddies' behalf, to provide the features described above:

  • Supabase (servers in Frankfurt, Germany): accounts, synced logbooks, Friends, profile photos, the player server, match data and reports.
  • Resend (USA): sending the sign-in code by email.
  • GitHub (USA): backup copy of the match data, and hosting this website.
  • API-Sports / api-sports.io: club logos and player photos; player data via the Weddies server.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, Apple Maps, App Store purchases and iCloud backup.
  • Google (Gmail): the mailbox that receives feedback emails.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, this happens on the basis of the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

14. Legal basis

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): your account, syncing your logbook, Friends, sign-in emails and player pages, because you ask for these features.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): security and preventing abuse (such as the hashed IP address and technical logs), handling reports and blocks, answering your messages and improving match and ground data.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): access to your location, camera and photos, the optional profile photo and the optional device details in feedback. You can withdraw consent at any time, for example in the iOS Settings app.

15. Security

  • All connections use encryption (HTTPS). Your sign-in session is stored in the iOS Keychain. Database rules ensure that only you can access your account data. Weddies collects as little data as possible.

16. Your rights

  • You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent.
  • Much of this you can do yourself in the app: change your data, export your logbook ("Export logbook (CSV)") and delete your account (Profile → Delete account).
  • For everything else, email kithavers@gmail.com. You will receive an answer within one month.
  • You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, such as the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl) or the authority in your own country.

17. Children

  • Weddies is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data. If you are under 16, only create an account with permission from a parent or guardian.
  • If you believe a child has created an account without permission, please email us and we will delete it.

18. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the release notes of the app update.
