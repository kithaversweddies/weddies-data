Last updated: 4 November 2026

With Friends in Weddies you can share your username, profile photo, club and statistics with friends you accept. Football is about rivalry, but on Weddies we keep it friendly. By creating a username you agree to these rules.

In short

  • Zero tolerance for offensive content and abusive users.
  • You can block and report anyone. We review reports within 24 hours.
  • If you break the rules, we remove the content and can block your account, without warning.

1. What is not allowed

  • Usernames or photos that are offensive, hateful, discriminatory, sexual, violent or threatening.
  • Harassment, bullying, intimidation or threats, also aimed at players, referees, clubs or supporters.
  • Pretending to be someone else, such as a player, club or another user.
  • Spam, advertising or fake accounts.
  • Personal information of other people (such as an address or phone number) in your username or photo.
  • Anything that is illegal or encourages illegal behaviour.

2. Block and report

Open a friend’s profile in Profile → Friends and tap ••• at the top right:

  • Block: you are no longer friends, they can’t find you or send you requests, and they won’t be told.
  • Report: choose a reason and send the report to Weddies. We look at every report within 24 hours.

3. What we do

When something breaks these rules, we remove the username or photo, and depending on how serious it is we can block the account or delete it. We may do this without warning. Reports are handled by the developer of Weddies; we never tell the person who reported them.

4. Contact

Questions, or something you want to report outside the app? Email kithavers@gmail.com.
